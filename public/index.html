About关于我

I'm Cary, based in California. I like turning a small idea into something people in a group chat actually use every day — a card to collect, a reminder that a stream just started, a song you can find again.

Most of what you see here was built together with AI coding tools, which I treat as a very patient teammate.

我是 Cary，住在加州。我喜欢把一个小点子变成群友每天真的会用的东西：一张想收集的卡、一条开播提醒、一首随时能找回来的歌。

这里的大部分东西都是和 AI 编程工具一起做的，我把它当成一个特别有耐心的队友。
